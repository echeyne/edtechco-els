\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Hierarchy recovery from documents.} Logical-structure recovery has a
long lineage --- pipelines, page-object datasets, layout-aware encoders,
OCR-free
models~\citep{lopez2009grobid,zhong2019publaynet,xu2020layoutlm,huang2022layoutlmv3,kim2022donut,blecher2023nougat}
--- but its targets are flat. Work that does reconstruct the tree parses
renderings, decomposes the task into page-object detection, reading order and
tree construction, or reads table-of-contents
metadata~\citep{rausch2021docparser,ma2023hrdoc,wang2024detect,wang2025unihdsa,xing2024dochienet,wehnert2025hips}.
All share a premise my setting violates: hierarchy can be \emph{learned},
because supervision exists over a genre whose typography is internally
consistent. Where every jurisdiction designs its own
document~\citep{ncecqa_elgs} there is neither. What these documents share is not
appearance but nesting, so my depth-map pass is functionally the
table-of-contents subtask of \citet{wang2024detect} --- inferred zero-shot from
content, then treated as the authority every element is classified against. The
nearest zero-shot neighbor~\citep{jeong2025docsray} also prompts for a pseudo
table of contents, but targets retrieval segmentation, not a typed hierarchy
carrying codes and ancestry.

\paragraph{Extraction without a labeled corpus.} Generative extraction is
competitive across classic IE tasks, especially where labeled corpora are
scarce~\citep{wadhwa2023revisiting,xu2024large,agrawal2022large,liu2022fewshot}.
GoLLIE~\citep{sainz2024gollie} is the closest analogue: training a model to
follow annotation \emph{guidelines} makes it generalize to unseen schemas. My
prompts do this without fine-tuning, stating document-agnostic structural
principles a frozen model applies to unseen states --- the available move rather
than the cheap one, since no annotated corpus of parsed early learning standards
exists. Three choices follow: schema-validated JSON with
deterministic repair rather than constrained
decoding~\citep{willard2023efficient}; domain-scoped chunking rather than
whole-document prompting, given positional degradation in long
contexts~\citep{liu2024lost}; and reported run-to-run
stability~\citep{wang2023selfconsistency} rather than a single run.
Hallucination~\citep{ji2023survey} I quantify by auditing every unmatched
detection by hand --- for hierarchical consistency, the analogue of the
internal-consistency checks \citet{aggarwal2025fiscal} apply to fiscal PDFs.
Absent labels, human effort goes to verification rather than annotation: every
record is reviewed at all four levels, following machine teaching rather than
active learning or selective
allocation~\citep{mosqueirarey2023human,monarch2021human,settles2009active,gilardi2023chatgpt,li2023coannotating,tan2024large}.
Review is blanket; confidence gates nothing.

\paragraph{Machine-readable education standards.} Two decades of work assume a
normalized set of standards already exists. ASN modeled US K--12 standards in
RDF~\citep{sutton2008achievement} and CASE defines an exchange
format~\citep{1edtech2017case}, but ASN was populated by hand and CASE
is a format, not an extractor; downstream work measures alignment and assigns
resources and items \emph{to} standards already
normalized~\citep{porter2002measuring,polikoff2011howwell,devaul2011computer,xu2025automated}.
For early childhood the assumption fails outright: no national framework spans
the age band~\citep{ohs2015elof} and each state publishes its own
PDF~\citep{ncecqa_elgs,ecs2024governance}. The step this paper automates is not
a competitor to that literature but its missing input.
