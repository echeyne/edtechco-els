\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Hierarchy recovery from documents.} Logical-structure recovery has a
long lineage: metadata pipelines, page-object datasets, layout-aware encoders,
and OCR-free
transcribers~\citep{lopez2009grobid,zhong2019publaynet,xu2020layoutlm,huang2022layoutlmv3,kim2022donut,blecher2023nougat}.
None of them targets a typed tree carrying codes and ancestry. Work that does
reconstruct the tree parses renderings, or decomposes the task into page-object
detection, reading order and tree
construction~\citep{rausch2021docparser,ma2023hrdoc,wang2024detect,wang2025unihdsa,xing2024dochienet}.
That line shares a premise my setting violates: hierarchy can be
\emph{learned}, because supervision exists over a genre whose typography is
internally consistent. Where every jurisdiction designs its own
document~\citep{ncecqa_elgs} there is neither. Two training-free neighbors
come closer. HiPS~\citep{wehnert2025hips} segments law books from
table-of-contents metadata, falling back to typographic cues refined by an LLM,
within one genre; DocsRay~\citep{jeong2025docsray} prompts for a pseudo table
of contents, but targets retrieval segmentation rather than a typed hierarchy
graded against structural annotations. What these documents share is not
appearance but nesting, so my depth-map pass is functionally the
table-of-contents subtask of \citet{wang2024detect}, inferred zero-shot from
content and then treated as the authority every element is classified against.

\paragraph{Extraction without a labeled corpus.} Generative extraction is
competitive across classic IE tasks, especially where labeled corpora are
scarce~\citep{wadhwa2023revisiting,xu2024large,agrawal2022large}, though where
labels do exist, parameter-efficient fine-tuning can beat in-context
learning~\citep{liu2022fewshot}. GoLLIE~\citep{sainz2024gollie} is the closest
analogue: training a model to follow annotation \emph{guidelines} makes it
generalize to unseen schemas. My prompts do this without fine-tuning, stating
document-agnostic structural principles a frozen model applies to unseen
states. That is the available move rather than the cheap one, since no
annotated corpus of parsed early learning standards exists. Three choices
follow: schema-validated JSON with deterministic repair rather than constrained
decoding~\citep{willard2023efficient}; domain-scoped chunking rather than
whole-document prompting, given positional degradation in long
contexts~\citep{liu2024lost}; and reported run-to-run
stability~\citep{wang2023selfconsistency} rather than a single run.
A generative extractor can emit content that is not in its source
\citep{ji2023survey}; I measure that directly, by auditing every unmatched
detection by hand rather than assuming it is a false positive. Structural
consistency is enforced separately, at the validation boundary, where an
indicator's code must extend its ancestor's; that check is the analogue of
the totals-at-each-level validation \citet{aggarwal2025fiscal} apply to
fiscal tables.
Absent labels, human effort goes to verification rather than annotation: every
record is reviewed at all four levels, in the manner of machine teaching rather
than active learning, uncertainty-guided allocation, or the model as
annotator~\citep{mosqueirarey2023human,monarch2021human,settles2009active,li2023coannotating,gilardi2023chatgpt,tan2024large}.
Review is blanket; confidence gates nothing.

\paragraph{Machine-readable education standards.} Two decades of work assume a
normalized set of standards already exists. ASN modeled US K--12 standards in
RDF~\citep{sutton2008achievement} and CASE defines an exchange
format~\citep{1edtech2017case}, but ASN was populated by hand and CASE
is a format, not an extractor; downstream work measures alignment and assigns
resources and items \emph{to} standards already
normalized~\citep{porter2002measuring,polikoff2011howwell,devaul2011computer,xu2025automated}.
For early childhood the assumption fails outright: the one federal framework
for the age band binds Head Start programs alone~\citep{ohs2015elof}, and each
state publishes its own PDF~\citep{ncecqa_elgs,ecs2024governance}. The step
this paper automates is not a competitor to that literature but its missing
input.
